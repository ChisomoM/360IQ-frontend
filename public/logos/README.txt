360IQ logo, PNG exports
Logo: Block. "360IQ" in Instrument Sans SemiBold, "IQ" in the blue to teal gradient.
Every logo file includes the clear space (the height of the "I") on all sides.

logo/
  horizontal-*      one-line lockup, 2400 px wide. Headers, documents, slides, email signatures.
  stacked-*         360 over IQ, 1600 px wide. Square spaces, covers, merchandise.
  on-white          white background (#FFFFFF), deep gradient
  on-paper          light grey background (#F4F7F9), deep gradient
  on-ink            dark background (#0B1B2B), bright gradient
  on-hero-gradient  the dark hero gradient background, bright gradient
  transparent-for-light   no background, for placing on light colours or photos with a light area
  transparent-for-dark    no background, for placing on dark colours
  mono-black / mono-white one colour, no background. Stamps, fax, single-colour print, embossing.

app-icon/
  1024, 512, 192    rounded ink tile (Android, PWA, web app manifest)
  180               rounded ink tile (Apple touch icon)
  1024-square       square corners, for app stores that round the icon themselves

favicon/
  64, 48, 32, 16    "IQ" on the ink tile. Use below 40 px, where "360" fills in.

social/
  social-avatar-1080  stacked logo on the hero gradient, square, for LinkedIn, Facebook and WhatsApp profile pictures.

Minimum sizes: one-line 72 px wide, stacked 40 px tall. Never re-type the logo in another font,
never put the gradient on "360", never stretch it. For print at large sizes, use the SVG files
in the design system (Assets, Logos).
